# Decisions before site-assessment automation

The blank manual worksheet is available now. These unresolved decisions prevent a defensible automated site evaluation or design; leaving them blank is intentional.

| Decision | Responsible reviewer | Evidence needed | Behavior until resolved |
|---|---|---|---|
| Intended assessment scope and qualified roles | Business owner / responsible professional | Written scope and verified relevant qualifications | Intake and evidence organization only |
| Applicable jurisdiction for each property | Responsible professional / authority | Parcel and boundary evidence; authority confirmation where needed | No county-name or lake-distance jurisdiction default |
| Field observations and methods | Responsible professional | Applicable evaluation requirements and approved field documentation | No soil, depth or rate presets |
| Current design rules and system applicability | Responsible designer / authority | Current rule/table/footnotes, local order and configuration | No automatic design-flow, tank, area or layout selection |
| Feature/component separation criteria | Responsible designer / authority | Applicable criteria and measurement basis for each pair | Blank distances; no generic setback pass/fail |
| Design approval and handoff | Responsible professional / authority | Required planning materials and approval record | Worksheet is not construction authorization |
| Business branding and customer records | Business owner | Approved contacts/credentials, retention/access and sharing policy | Project name only; completed records kept private |

Any future implementation needs representative synthetic cases reviewed against the chosen source editions, including missing evidence and unsupported system types. It must keep mapped data separate from measurements and distinguish unresolved inputs from zero.
